📖 About Yamal

Lamine Yamal is a Spanish footballer who developed through Barcelona's famous La Masia academy. He is known for his close control, creative passing, quick dribbling and ability to cut inside from the right wing. He made his Barcelona first-team debut in 2023 at just 15 years old. In 2024, he helped Spain win the European Championship and received the tournament's Young Player award.
