About Messi

Lionel Messi is an Argentine footballer celebrated for his close control, dribbling, passing, creativity and finishing. He developed at Barcelona, played for Paris Saint-Germain, and joined Inter Miami in 2023. With Argentina, he won the 2021 and 2024 Copa América titles and the 2022 FIFA World Cup.
