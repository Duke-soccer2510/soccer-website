📖 About Ronaldo

Cristiano Ronaldo is a Portuguese footballer known for his powerful shooting, aerial ability, speed and remarkable goalscoring record. He has played for Sporting CP, Manchester United, Real Madrid, Juventus and Al Nassr. He won major trophies in England, Spain, Italy and Saudi Arabia, as well as the European Championship with Portugal in 2016.
