📖 About Mbappé

Kylian Mbappé is a French forward known for his explosive pace, dribbling and finishing. He rose to prominence at Monaco before becoming a star at Paris Saint-Germain. He joined Real Madrid in 2024. With France, he won the 2018 FIFA World Cup and scored a hat-trick in the 2022 World Cup final.
